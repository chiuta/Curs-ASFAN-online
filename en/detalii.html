About the course

ASFAN – the Association for the Study of Unidentified Aerospace Phenomena – invites you to the course “Introduction to Ufology” (fourth series).

Invitation

The course covers the basics of unidentified aerospace phenomena: the latest political and military developments, disinformation, abductions, folkloric, religious and paranormal connections, shamans, attempts at explanation, investigation methodology, false UFOs, international organisations in the field, and more.

The course runs via ZOOM, generally every two weeks on Wednesdays from 19:00 to 21:00, with one hour of presentation and one hour of discussion, between 18 November 2026 and 26 May 2027, avoiding public holidays and student exam periods.

Participation is free, and at the end of the course, participants with no more than three absences may request a diploma.

Places are limited. Enrol by sending an e-mail to adrian.urzica@gmail.com by 17 November 2026. Those admitted (generally in order of application) will receive the ZOOM link for each talk at their e-mail address.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to take part?

Participation is free.

How do I get the ZOOM link?

Those admitted receive the ZOOM link for each talk at their e-mail address.

How do I enrol?

By sending an e-mail to adrian.urzica@gmail.com by 17 November 2026. You can also use the form on the enrolment page, which prepares the e-mail for you.

In what order are participants admitted?

Places are limited; those admitted are, in principle, chosen in order of application.

Can I get a diploma?

Yes. At the end of the course, participants with no more than three absences may request a diploma.

How long is a session and when does it take place?

On Wednesdays, between 19:00 and 21:00: one hour of presentation and one hour of discussion. Sessions take place, in principle, every two weeks, avoiding public holidays and student exam periods.

What topics are covered?

The latest political and military developments, disinformation, abductions, folkloric, religious and paranormal connections, shamans, attempts at explanation, investigation methodology, false UFOs, international organisations in the field, and more.

In which language is the course held?

The course is held in Romanian (presentations and discussions). This website is translated into 8 languages for information only.
