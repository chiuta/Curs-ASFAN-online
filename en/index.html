Association for the Study of Unidentified Aerospace Phenomena (ASFAN)

INVITATION

ASFAN invites you to take part in a course entitled

INTRODUCTION TO UFOLOGY

(fourth series)

The course covers the basics of unidentified aerospace phenomena: the latest political and military developments, disinformation, abductions, folkloric, religious and paranormal connections, shamans, attempts at explanation, investigation methodology, false UFOs, international organisations in the field, and more.

The course runs via ZOOM, generally every two weeks on Wednesdays from 19:00 to 21:00, with one hour of presentation and one hour of discussion, between 18 November 2026 and 26 May 2027, avoiding public holidays and student exam periods.

Participation is free, and at the end of the course, participants with no more than three absences may request a diploma.

Places are limited. Enrol by sending an e-mail to adrian.urzica@gmail.com by 17 November 2026. Those admitted (generally in order of application) will receive the ZOOM link for each talk at their e-mail address.

Introduction to ufology

ASFAN course, Nov 2026 – May 2027 · proposed structure

📅 Add the whole course to your calendar

  1. Wednesday 18 November 202619:00–21:00

    Unidentified aerospace/anomalous phenomena (UAP/UFO), the basics: aerial, underwater, outer space, transmedium. Daytime, nighttime and radar sightings. Characteristics, landings. Aggressive UFOs.

    📅 Calendar
  2. Wednesday 2 December 202619:00–21:00

    Political-military history 1942-2017, nuclear connections, crashes, conflicts, projects, Blue Book, Hynek, regulations. Secrecy, disinformation, taboo, ridicule, media complicity, the UFO mythology. Also among the Russians, British, French, South Americans etc.

    📅 Calendar
  3. Wednesday 16 December 202619:00–21:00

    After 2017: Congress, the Pentagon, NASA, AARO etc. Private initiatives. Has the American military industry used UFO debris? Russia, China and ICER, other associations. Official UFO structures. The latest declassifications.

    📅 Calendar
  4. Wednesday 13 January 202719:00–21:00

    Close encounters of the 3rd-4th kind. Contacts, contactees, abductions, scenario, genetic experiments, indoctrination, special characteristics, evidence, abductions without return. Hopkins, Mack etc. Mutilations, cryptozoology, reservations?

    📅 Calendar
  5. Wednesday 24 February 202719:00–21:00

    The ancient history of the phenomenon, folkloric connections, iele (Romanian folklore fairies), dwarves, Magonia, magic circles and crop circles, Vallée.

    📅 Calendar
  6. Wednesday 3 March 202719:00–21:00

    The connection with the paranormal. The CRV remote viewing technique, channeling, Akasha, reincarnations? Spiritual aspects, journeys of the spirit, shamans, techniques, Mack.

    📅 Calendar
  7. Wednesday 10 March 202719:00–21:00

    Remarkable Romanian cases. History, ufologists, associations, magazines.

    📅 Calendar
  8. Wednesday 24 March 202719:00–21:00

    Religious connections, sects, holy apparitions, UFOs in the Bible?, heavenly abductions, genetic experiments, satanic? Vatican, Vallée.

    📅 Calendar
  9. Wednesday 31 March 202719:00–21:00

    Ancient civilisations, Silurian, Atlantis, Nibiru, Anunnaki, Pleiadians, reptilians. Paleocontact, ooparts, cyclopean constructions, mummies.

    📅 Calendar
  10. Wednesday 7 April 202719:00–21:00

    Possible explanations: time travellers? Intraterrestrials? Etc. Two extraterrestrial hypotheses, hypercivilisations, conditions, self-destruction, non-intervention. Cultural hypnosis, limits, possible consequences.

    📅 Calendar
  11. Wednesday 21 April 202719:00–21:00

    Attempts at disclosure. Declassifications. Will there be a contact? What will it look like? Under what conditions? The current attitude towards disclosure: military, researchers, religious people, the general public.

    📅 Calendar
  12. Wednesday 28 April 202719:00–21:00

    More Romanian cases. Open presentations. Films, photographs etc.

    📅 Calendar
  13. Wednesday 12 May 202719:00–21:00

    Methodology, how we investigate UAP cases, ASFAN resources (database, data archive etc.). How to recognise/tell apart a UFO, false UFOs, aircraft, satellites, photographs and films, fakes.

    📅 Calendar
  14. Wednesday 26 May 202719:00–21:00

    Conclusions, questions and final discussion.
